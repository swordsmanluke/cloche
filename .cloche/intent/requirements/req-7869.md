A DRA may only list tasks/runs of its project, read run status/output, create tasks (always labelled with its domain) and update/close tasks carrying its domain label; stop, delete, resume, shutdown, loop control, project purge, signal tokens and other projects are refused.

**Why:** Least privilege for autonomous agents: it manages its own domain's tasks and runs do the work.
