A judge step only answers a Saga question by applying a ruling already on record (a Saga ruling in force or an intent entry), citing its id; if unsure it holds the question for the human. A judge answer is refused if the question already has a ruling, and the human overrules with `--supersedes`.

**Why:** Judges must never invent new rulings and must never override a human.
