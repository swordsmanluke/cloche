A signal's source+key is stored on the tracker task as a `signal:<source>:<hash>` label so update-task action=find can locate the open task and a repeat signal attaches a comment instead of creating a duplicate task.

**Why:** Dedup of repeat signals relies on the label convention across tracker modules.
