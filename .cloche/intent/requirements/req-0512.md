The task-stack's Done group has no age boundary: it shows every completed task, newest-first, one page at a time (default page_size 25, max 100 via the `page_size` query param), rather than being scoped to a calendar day.

**Why:** The prior "Done today" design bounded the group to the daemon-local calendar day, which required computing a day boundary in the daemon's local time zone; that boundary itself was the source of a UTC-midnight bug (cloche-eiil.24). Removing the age cutoff entirely removes the boundary calculation instead of just fixing it.
