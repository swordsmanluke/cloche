The daemon-wide budget cap is a single percentage of the subscription usage allowance per day (not per project, no per-window cap), counting all account usage. When hit: no new runs launch (loop, timed work, DRA wake-ups) but manual `cloche run` still works with a warning; in-flight runs get a `grace` period then are parked (resumable via `cloche resume`), not killed; launching resumes automatically when usage drops under the cap. `emergency_overflow` decides whether emergency tasks may launch past the cap.

**Why:** Unattended running must not drain the subscription allowance; parking preserves work.
