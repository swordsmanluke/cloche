Events arriving for a DRA while it is awake or the budget gate is closed are kept and delivered together in its next wake-up; nothing is dropped, nothing starts a second run, and a wake caused by the DRA's own action must not wake it again.

**Why:** Prevents lost events and self-triggering wake loops (ruling CLOCHE-A123).
