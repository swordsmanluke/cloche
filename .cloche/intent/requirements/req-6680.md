A `user_edited` requirement's statement and scope are never rewritten in place by a scan; the scan may only supersede it with a new requirement, leaving the user's original text intact in history.

**Why:** Protects manual edits from being silently overwritten by extraction — supersession preserves an audit trail instead of mutating what the user wrote.
