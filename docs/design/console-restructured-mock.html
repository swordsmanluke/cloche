The Console, restructured

Projects are tabs across the top. A narrow stack of tasks on the left, grouped by what they need from you. The whole centre belongs to the selected task: a horizontal step strip, then a log that gets every remaining pixel. The daemon's instruments sit in the tab bar and the activity stream collapses to a ticker at the foot.

Try it: click fnn6, usjb or hkhg in the stack. The step strip, log and actions change with the task's state.

Stack contents, attempt ids, durations, tokens and step outcomes are live state from this machine. The log bodies, tool lines, container id, diff stat and the compare view's error text are illustrative; they show the width the real content needs rather than the real content.

How the space is spent

  • Tab bar = projects + daemon. Each tab carries health, running count and an attention flag; idle projects fold into a menu. Loop, slots, queue and burn sit at the right of the same bar.
  • Stack = one column, 272 px. Grouped Needs you · Running · Queued · Done today, then "earlier" as a bounded scroll-back. Two-line clamp on titles, elapsed on the right.
  • Centre = the selected task. A one-line header, a facts row with attempt tabs, a horizontal step strip that inlines the child run's steps, then the log at full width. Nothing else competes for it.
  • Activity is a ticker, one line at the foot, expandable with a. It is the same stream the earlier Console mock gave a whole panel to.

What the state changes

  • Running: live-following log with a type filter; actions are console, workflow, cancel.
  • Needs you: a why-line, then the log pane becomes a three-column compare aligned on the first failure; actions are release, close, re-run.
  • Done: branch and merge facts, the finalize log, and the failed first attempt one keystroke away; actions are branch, diff, delete container.
  • Parked (weave): the log pane would become the help-thread transcript with a reply box. Not mocked.

Cost and reuse

  • URL scheme /{project}/{task}; attempt and step in the query. Deep links work, back button works.
  • Reuses the SSE log stream, the flattened step tree, the attempts API and the per-step output endpoint as they are.
  • New from the daemon: slot and queue occupancy, the attention model (parked, stale, repeat-failure, long poll), and grouping of user-initiated built-in runs.
  • Workflows, Intent and Containers become secondary views reachable from the header actions, not top-level pages.
  • This layout is also a legitimate TUI; the keys in the foot are the same either way.
